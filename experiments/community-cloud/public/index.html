CONNECT IN YOUR BROWSER
Open official HoYoLAB ↗

Sign in on hoyolab.com, not on Istaroth. HoYoLAB does not offer us third-party OAuth; for live resin, Community needs your authenticated session.

Enter the session cookie values from your own HoYoLAB login. Never enter your HoYoLAB password or email verification code here.

Where do I get my session?

In a desktop browser signed into HoYoLAB, open Developer Tools → Application/Storage → Cookies → hoyolab.com and copy the ltuid_v2 and ltoken_v2 cookie names and values as ltuid_v2=...; ltoken_v2=.... Some accounts also need ltmid_v2. Do not share these values in chats.

Most phone browsers, especially Safari on iPhone, do not expose these cookies. Signing in to HoYoLAB in another tab alone cannot pair the accounts; this method may be impractical without a browser that lets you access your own session.

Connect using the open-source GitHub browser helper

Optional helper for supported browsers. The browser extension can read HoYoLAB cookies after permission; a bookmarklet can only read non-HttpOnly cookies and will usually not work on iPhone Safari.
