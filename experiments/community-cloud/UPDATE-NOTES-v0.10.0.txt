Istaroth Community v0.10.0 — WEB PAIRING ONLY

Open the Community website, sign in with Telegram, and use the new website HoYoLAB connection panel.
Users provide their OWN HoYoLAB session cookie header to this website (no password entry).
The Worker checks the session, detects linked Genshin accounts, and asks for explicit consent before encrypted storage.
No Windows Istaroth, Android connector, iOS connector, or standalone mobile app is required for the connection interface.

Important limitation: HoYoLAB does not offer an official third-party OAuth flow for resin.
Normal browsers cannot read HoYoLAB cookies from another website. Obtaining the required session on phones may be difficult, especially on iOS Safari; simply signing in in another tab is NOT enough. Never enter a HoYoLAB password or verification code into the Community website.

Existing Windows-connected and phone-estimate account records are retained. Existing database, encryption key, and Telegram settings stay untouched by the updater.
Telegram outbound delivery is still separate and must be tested on the live Cloudflare installation.
